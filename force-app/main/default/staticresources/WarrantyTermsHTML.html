Exclusions: Wear and Tear Coverage does not apply to gradual deterioration that has not resulted in complete mechanical breakdown, reduced performance, noise, vibration, minor seepage or leakage, carbon buildup, components operating within or exceeding manufacturer tolerances without actual failure, or parts recommended for replacement as part of routine maintenance, including but not limited to tires, brake friction materials and linings, clutch friction materials, belts, hoses, filters, fluids, batteries, spark plugs, light bulbs, and wiper blades.

PART 6: MAXIMUM AUTHORIZED REPAIR LIMIT

6.1 MAXIMUM AUTHORIZED REPAIR LIMIT – The liabilities of A-Protect under this service contract are determined solely by the coverage selected on the front page of the Application and the premium paid at the time of purchase. The maximum liability per claim shall not exceed half of the purchase price of the Covered Vehicle, and the total aggregate liability under this Contract shall not exceed the purchase price of the covered vehicle (excluding taxes, fees, and any aftermarket product). When multiple covered component failures are diagnosed at the same time, all related repairs will be considered a single authorized service event and applied toward the same claim limit. All amounts authorized and paid by A-Protect to the repair facility are inclusive of applicable taxes.

6.2 DEDUCTIBLE – For each authorized repair event, the Customer shall be responsible for payment of the deductible amount indicated on the Registration Page of this Service Contract. The deductible must be paid directly to the A-Protect Authorized Repair Facility at the time of repair. If multiple covered components are repaired or replaced during the same authorized service visit, only one deductible shall apply. The deductible does not apply to any portion of costs that A-Protect determines to be outside the scope of this Service Contract, nor to non-covered parts, fluids, or incidental expenses. All authorized payments are administrative in nature and do not constitute the settlement of an insurance claim or indemnification for loss.

16. A-Protect is not responsible for delays in completing repairs or for losses resulting from such delays. 17. Clutch failure in manual or sequential transmissions is not classified as a transmission failure under this Service Contract.

18. The timing belt is not covered as a standalone repair.

19. The arrangement of third-party towing or roadside services for delivery of the vehicle to an A-Protect Authorized Repair Facility, or otherwise, is not covered.

20. Damage or loss resulting from the Customer’s failure to perform maintenance in accordance with the A-Protect Maintenance Schedule is excluded.

21. Any loss or damage caused by the lack of proper lubricants or coolants, including failure of water hoses, radiators, their connections, or transmission oil cooler lines, is not covered.

22. Normal wear and tear is excluded. This includes, but is not limited to, repair or replacement of valves, valve seals, or piston rings causing high oil consumption or low compression, stretched timing chains, noisy lifters, and worn bearings.

23. Repairs to carbonized or burned valves or seized piston rings are excluded.

24. Risks, losses, or damage resulting from rust, corrosion, contamination, loss of fluids, lubricants, antifreeze, or refrigerants, improper type of engine oil or fuel, or any failure caused by burning oil or overheating, regardless of cause, are not covered.

25. Gasoline exhaust systems and diesel exhaust components or fluids, including BlueTEC and AdBlue, are excluded from coverage.

26. Emission system components, including but not limited to the evaporative emission system, exhaust gas recirculation system, positive crankcase ventilation system, and catalytic converters, are excluded.

27. A-Protect shall not be liable for any damage, injury, or death, including any costs, expenses, or legal claims, suffered by any person, firm, or corporation, whether directly or indirectly caused by the vehicle or its components.

28. Risks, losses, or damages resulting from misdiagnosis, improper repair, abuse, negligence, acts of God, repossession, collision, fire, theft, water damage, or freezing are not covered.
