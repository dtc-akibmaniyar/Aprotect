The services available under this Service Contract are discretionary service benefits administered by A-Protect and depend on the selections made on the front page of the Application. Coverage applies only to the Registered Vehicle identified on the Application and only during the active Term of this Service Contract. All reimbursements or services are discretionary and are subject to prior authorization by A-Protect, verification of incident details, and submission of valid paid receipts within fourteen (14) days of the occurrence. A-Protect reserves the sole right to inspect the damage, request additional documentation (including photographs or inspection reports), and determine eligibility prior to any approval of reimbursement. Repairs or replacements performed without prior authorization may be denied. Eligibility Requirements: The vehicle must not exceed ten (10) model years prior to the current model year at the time of sale.

2.1 Maximum Liability Limits – This Service Contract shall carry a maximum liability limit which shall not exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000 CAD) or the verified repair value of the Covered Vehicle, whichever amount is less, at the time of an approved covered repair. The per-claim maximum limit applicable to each individual covered service is outlined in Part 3 of this Service Contract. All reimbursements or approved repairs are subject to prior authorization by A-Protect and are provided at A-Protect’s sole discretion, in accordance with the terms of this Service Contract. Multiple repairs diagnosed at the same time constitute a single claim.

PART 3: PARTS & SERVICES COVERED

Protection Plan Selected: Refer to the Application Page for the Protection Plan (Essential, Extended or Superior), term, and any surcharges or add-ons selected.

*All repairs and reimbursements are at the discretion of A-Protect. The Administrator may select parts that are new, rebuilt, or equivalent quality. All exchanged parts removed by A-Protect or the service centre will remain the property of A-Protect.

Essential Protection (Section 3.1–3.3) includes:

3.1 Tire & Wheel Coverage

a) Flat Tire Replacement: A-Protect may reimburse the customer up to a maximum of $80 per occurrence for the repair of a flat tire caused by a road hazard, provided the covered vehicle was operated lawfully at the time of the incident.

b) Tire Replacement: A-Protect may, at its sole discretion, reimburse the Customer for the reasonable costs to replace up to two (2) tires per occurrence when a Covered Tire becomes Unserviceable as a direct result of a Road Hazard, as defined in this Service Contract. A tire is considered Unserviceable only when it cannot be safely repaired, as determined by A-Protect. Reimbursement may be made up to a maximum of two thousand dollars ($2,000 CAD) for the entire Term of this Service Contract. Replacement tires will be of “like kind and quality,” which may include new or used tires and may involve a prorated adjustment based on the remaining tread depth of the damaged tire. If the Covered Vehicle is equipped with factory-installed run-flat tires, the maximum reimbursement limit may increase by up to three thousand dollars ($3,000 CAD) for the Term of this Service Contract, due to the higher cost of run-flat tire replacements. Note: Snow Tires included.

c) Wheel Replacement: A-Protect may, at its discretion, reimburse the Customer for reasonable costs to repair or replace an Alloy Wheel that has been rendered Unserviceable as a direct result of a Road Hazard, as defined in this Service Contract. Coverage is limited to the replacement of up to two (2) Alloy Wheels per occurrence, with a maximum of four (4) Alloy Wheels during the term of this Service Contract. A-Protect reserves the right to supply or authorize replacement wheels of like kind and quality, which may include new, refurbished, or used components, at A-Protect’s cost and discretion. Bent Alloy Wheels are eligible for consideration only when the damage was caused by a verified Road Hazard, and the wheel cannot properly seal with its tire as determined by an authorized technician and A-Protect.

3.2 Tire & Wheel Mounting and Balancing

A-Protect will reimburse the Customer for the reasonable costs for mounting, balancing, valve stems, and tire disposal related to Covered Tire replacements. Unspecified shop fees, environmental charges, and other miscellaneous supplies are excluded.

3.3 Roadside Coverage

For the Registered Vehicle, A-Protect will reimburse the Customer up to $100 per occurrence for roadside assistance services, limited to: towing (to the nearest A-Protect approved service center), winching, flat tire change (installation of inflated spare; spare not included), fuel delivery (excluding cost of fuel), battery boost, lockout service (excluding locksmith fees). Roadside Assistance is limited to three (3) service calls per twelve (12) month period, and no more than one (1) service call within any twenty-four (24) hour period. Coverage is invalid following Termination of the Service Contract. Valid invoices and proof of payment must be submitted within fourteen (14) days of the occurrence to be eligible for reimbursement.

A-Protect does not arrange or dispatch roadside services, reimbursement only.

Extended Protection (Section 3.1–3.5) covers all of Essential Protection in addition to:

3.4 Key Replacement

In the event the original key (issued at the time of vehicle purchase) is lost, stolen, or destroyed, A-Protect will reimburse up to $800 per Covered Year and shall not exceed $1,600 over the Term of the Service Contract for the reasonable costs to replace a key/remote. Prior authorization from A-Protect is required before any replacement is made. Non-working keys must be available for inspection at A-Protect’s discretion. The Selling Dealership must provide two (2) keys and two (2) remotes at the time of vehicle delivery.

3.5 Car Rental Coverage

In the event that a covered repair or replacement under this Service Contract requires more than one (1) business day to complete, A-Protect may, at its discretion, reimburse the Customer up to seventy dollars ($70 CAD) per day for reasonable rental-vehicle expenses. All reimbursements are subject to prior authorization by A-Protect and submission of valid paid receipts within fourteen (14) days of the occurrence.

This assistance is administrative in nature and does not constitute insurance coverage or indemnification for loss of use of the vehicle.

Superior Protection (Section 3.1–3.5) covers all of Essential and Extended Protection in addition to:

3.6 Windshield Repair and Replacement

A‑Protect will reimburse the Customer for the cost of repairs to minor chips and cracks on the vehicle’s windshield that result directly from damage caused by road hazards, including but not limited to propelled rocks, wood, and metal debris. The maximum reimbursement under this coverage shall be three hundred dollars ($300) per occurrence, not to exceed a total aggregate limit of one thousand dollars ($1,000) over the term of this Service Contract. In the event that a full windshield replacement is required due to such damage, A‑Protect will reimburse the Customer up to one thousand dollars ($1,000) toward the applicable windshield replacement deductible. This replacement coverage is limited to one (1) claim under the term of this Service Contract. Cracks exceeding fifteen (15) centimeters in length are expressly excluded from coverage.

3.7 Headlight, Taillight, Fog Light/Driving Light Lens Repair

Reimbursement for headlight, taillight, fog light, and driving light lenses for minor chips and cracks only, as a direct result of damage due to road objects, such as propelled rocks or debris (wood, metal pieces/parts), A-Protect will cover up to $150.00 per occurrence or the maximum of $600.00 for the total term of the Service Contract. Exclusions: Cracks over 15 cm are not covered.

3.8 Paintless Dent and Scratch Repair

A-Protect may, at its discretion, reimburse the Customer for reasonable costs up to two hundred fifty dollars ($250 CAD) per occurrence, to a maximum of one thousand dollars ($1,000 CAD) over the term of this Service Contract. Coverage applies only to minor dents up to five (5) centimetres in diameter or surface scratches up to thirty (30) centimetres in length on external body panels, when the damage is caused directly by public-lot or parking-area incidents, as determined by A‑Protect. Repairs are performed using the Paintless Dent Repair method, where feasible, and are intended to restore the appearance as closely as possible to the original finish. Exact colour or texture matching is not guaranteed. A-Protect reserves the sole right to determine repair eligibility, select the repair facility, and authorize the reimbursement amount.

3.9 Rip/Tear/Burn/Puncture

For the repair of accidental rips, tears, burns, or punctures up to 3 cm in length for the upholstered seats of the Covered vehicle, A-Protect will cover up to $150.00 per occurrence or the maximum of $600.00 for the total term of the Service Contract.

PART 4: CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

4.1 Regular Vehicle Maintenance – During the Coverage Term of this Service Contract, the Customer shall ensure that all services required to maintain the Covered Vehicle are performed by a licensed repair facility. This includes, but is not limited to, conducting regular tire rotations, maintaining proper inflation levels, and performing timely wheel alignments. The Customer shall also ensure that any mechanical or structural conditions that may affect the performance or integrity of the tires and rims, such as suspension, steering, or wheel balance issues are promptly inspected and repaired when identified. Regular maintenance expenses, including the cost of inspections, adjustments, alignments, balancing, tire rotations, or any other routine services, are expressly excluded from coverage under this Service Contract. Failure to perform, or delay in performing, such maintenance or repairs that results in damage to the tires, rims, or related components shall void coverage for such damage under this Service Contract.

4.2 Vehicle Operation/Safe Driving Practices – The Customer shall operate the Covered Vehicle in a safe, lawful, and prudent manner to prevent avoidable damage to covered components. The Customer shall exercise reasonable care to avoid contact with potholes, curbs, debris, and other road hazards that may cause damage to the tires or rims. Any damage resulting from reckless, negligent, or improper vehicle operation shall not be covered under this Service Contract.

4.3 Claim Reporting & Procedure – The Customer shall comply with the claim procedures set forth under Part 5 of this Service Contract and shall promptly report any damage to the covered tires or rims to A-Protect within a reasonable time following the occurrence. Failure to provide timely notice or to follow the prescribed claim procedures may result in the denial of coverage. A-Protect reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to request photos/videos, or other supporting documentation evidencing the damage at the time of claim submission.

B. TIRE AND WHEEL DAMAGE EXCLUSIONS

Repairs or replacements for tires and/or Alloy Wheels are excluded when, in A-Protect’s sole discretion, the damage is determined to be:

Not a direct result of a covered Road Hazard as defined in Part 1;

Minor or cosmetic in nature and does not render the tire or wheel Unserviceable;

Beyond the capacity to be safely repaired; or

Pre-existing or resulting from a defect or condition that existed before the Effective Date.

Additional exclusions include:

Tires or wheels covered by a manufacturer’s Service Contract, goodwill adjustment, recall, or service campaign;

Damage caused by improper inflation, alignment, suspension defects, or mechanical failure;

Tires with tread depth of 3/32" (2.4 mm) or less at the time of damage;

Damage to tires or wheels transferred from another vehicle after the Effective Date;

Vehicles with Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) greater than 13,600 kg (30 000 lbs);

Damage from collision, accident avoidance, or any impact affecting body, suspension, or frame components.

If wheel damage is extensive but the wheel still seals properly with its tire, replacement may be considered at A-Protect’s discretion.

C. PAINTLESS DENT REPAIR (PDR) AND APPEARANCE LIMITATIONS

The PDR process is not applicable to edges or areas where manufacturer bracing prevents tool access.

Repairs to creased metal or areas where the paint finish is cracked, chipped, or otherwise damaged.

Repairs where the PDR process could further damage the paint or finish.

Plastic panels, bumpers, or trim pieces are excluded.

Cosmetic refinishing is limited to industry-standard reconditioning; exact colour or texture matching is not guaranteed.

D. GLASS, FABRIC AND INTERIOR EXCLUSIONS

Only minor, repairable chips or cracks caused directly by road-hazard debris are eligible.

Stress cracks exceeding fifteen (15) centimetres are excluded.

Rips, tears, burns, or punctures that cannot be safely repaired without further damage are excluded.

Interior wear, fading, or discoloration due to age, sunlight, or chemical exposure is not covered.

E. OPERATIONAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL EXCLUSIONS

Damage resulting from improper maintenance, misuse, negligence, or abuse.

Corrosion, rust, oxidation, or damage from salt, chemicals, or cleaning products.

Damage occurring while the vehicle is used for commercial, rideshare, or delivery purposes (including Uber, Lyft, Skip the Dishes, taxi, courier, limousine, emergency, snowplow, racing, or driving school use).

Damage caused by Acts of God such as hail, flood, freezing, lightning, or other natural disasters.

Damage or loss due to collision, theft, vandalism, fire, or water intrusion.

F. ADMINISTRATIVE AND LEGAL EXCLUSIONS

False or fraudulent claims, misrepresentation, or submission of falsified documentation.

Unauthorized or “Do-It-Yourself (DIY)” repairs or replacements.

Misdiagnosis or improper repair by any facility not authorized by A-Protect.

Damage, injury, or death to any person or entity, and all related legal or incidental costs.

Vehicles not registered for private passenger use or any vehicle exceeding the GVWR limit above.

Loss or damage resulting from confiscation, repossession, or seizure under legal authority.

Claims occurring outside of Canada.
